Frequently asked questions

Is this QR code generator free?

Yes. It is completely free with no sign-up, no account, and no usage limits.

Do the QR codes expire?

No. The QR codes are static and encode your URL or text directly, so they never expire and there is no redirect that can break.

Is my data tracked or uploaded?

No. The generator runs entirely in your browser. The text you enter is never sent to a server, so it stays private.

Can I customize the QR code size and colors?

Yes. You can choose the size (128px to 400px), set custom dark and light colors, and pick an error-correction level before downloading the code as a PNG.

Can I use the QR codes commercially?

Yes. The generated QR codes are yours to use for any purpose, including commercial use, with no attribution required.
